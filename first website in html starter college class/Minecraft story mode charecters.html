Jessie

Jesse is the playable main protagonist in Minecraft: Story Mode developed by Telltale Games, whose gender and appearance is chosen by the player with the male version being voiced by Patton Oswalt and the female by Catherine Anne "Cat" Taber. Jesse makes their official debut in "The Order of the Stone". Initially, Jesse participated as a former competitive builder alongside their friends, Axel, Olivia, and their pet pig Reuben.

Axel

Axel is a major character in Minecraft: Story Mode developed by Telltale Games. He is a large, strong human who is known to be a prankster/jokester. And is one of Jesse's closest friends. Axel makes his first appearance in "The Order of The Stone as a former competitive builder, eventually joining The new Order of The Stone. He is voiced by Brian Posehn.

Olivia

Olivia is a major character in Minecraft: Story Mode,developed by Telltale Games. A skilled builder with a particular expertise in Redstone,she is one of Jesse's closest friends. Olivia makes her first appearance in "The Order of the Stone" as a competitive builder,and later becomes a member of the New Order of the Stone.

Petra

Petra is the deuteragonist of Minecraft: Story Mode developed by Telltale Games, She is a fighter who has been known as very determined and tough. She often exchanged certain items for treasures before becoming a member of the New Order of the stone. Before she was a member of Jesse's Gang, she was a mercenary living in caves that was known as mysterious and cool yet inapproachable due to her toughness. Petra made her first appearance in "The Order of the Stone". She is voiced by Ashley Johnson.

Lukas

Lukas is the tritagonist of the Minecraft: Story Mode series developed by Telltale Games. He makes his first appearance In "The Order of the Stone." As the leader of The Ocelots, a team of builders that served as the rivals to Jesse's Gang. His original group in the first episode had Adien (the main bully in the group), Gill, and Maya, then in episode 5 Adien, Gill, and Maya made their own group called the blaze rods and after that in season to Lukas remade The Ocelot from previos charecters in season 1.
